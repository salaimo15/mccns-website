Measured on board

Every sail starts on your boat

We take the measurements on board, check them against the rig, and agree the cloth, cut and details with you before anything is built.

What we do

Sails, from new to repaired

  • New sailsMainsails, genoas, jibs, spinnakers and gennakers.
  • On board measurementLuff, leech and foot taken on your boat, not from a catalogue.
  • RepairsStitching, panel replacement, reef points, leech lines and UV strips.
  • Service and storageInspection, washing and winter storage between seasons.
  • Covers and canvasSail covers, lazy bags and protective canvas work.
  • Fitting and tuningDelivery, hoisting and setup on the boat.

Bow to keel

One team for the whole boat

We plan and run the work, from routine upkeep to complete refits, and keep the yacht in order between voyages.

What we do

Managed, maintained, refitted

  • Yacht managementMaintenance planning, supervision and owner reporting.
  • Complete refitsInterior, deck and systems, project managed end to end.
  • Seasonal preparationCommissioning before the season, laying up after it.
  • Rigging and deck gearInspection and replacement of standing and running rigging.
  • Refit planningSpecifications, quotations and schedules agreed before work starts.
  • Yard coordinationHaul out, berths and contractors arranged for the owner.
